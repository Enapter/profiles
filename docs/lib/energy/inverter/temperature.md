# Inverter Temperature

Provides temperature measurements for different parts of the inverter.

## Telemetry

### `inverter_heatsink_temperature`

- **Display name:** Heatsink Temperature
- **Type:** `float`
- **Unit:** Cel

Temperature of the inverter's heatsink.

### `inverter_internal_temperature`

- **Display name:** Internal Temperature
- **Type:** `float`
- **Unit:** Cel

Internal temperature of the inverter.
